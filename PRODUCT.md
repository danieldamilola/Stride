# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Power users, developers, and casual users seeking focus and minimalism. They are looking for a calm workspace to replace standard, noisy browsers.

## Product Purpose
A fast, minimalist browser that strips away visual noise and bloat. It provides a focused digital environment without distractions.

## Brand Personality
Calm, minimalist, focused.

## Anti-references
Generic AI slop, SaaS-cream (warm-tinted generic templates), overly saturated colors, emoji UI icons. It should explicitly avoid looking like it was generated by an AI default template.

## Design Principles
1. **Calmness over flash:** Interfaces should reduce cognitive load, not add to it.
2. **Authentic materials:** Use real SVGs, precise typography, and intentional spacing instead of cheap tricks like emojis or excessive blur.
3. **Show, don't tell:** Onboarding should involve configuring real, useful settings (like importing data) rather than just reading feature cards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG contrast. High readability.
